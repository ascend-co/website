III. Process

From the first call through the closing and beyond.

01

We get to know each other

Discussions this important shouldn't happen over email. If there's mutual interest following an initial call, we'd like to schedule a site visit to see your shop, meet your team, and get to know you. We want to like and trust who we are dealing with, and you should expect the same.

02

Make a deal that works

We can be flexible and creative to put forward a deal that meets your goals and timeline, whether that's a near-term retirement, a multi-year transition, or staying on as an owner alongside us.

03

Grow it from there

We roll up our sleeves and get to work, taking what we can off your plate so you and your team can focus on the parts of the business you enjoy most.
